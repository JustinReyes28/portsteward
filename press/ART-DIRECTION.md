# Signal Ledger

Large, calm fields establish order before the eye reaches the detail. One strong typographic gesture gives the composition its weight; the supporting marks stay small and exact. Careful alignment and generous margins should make the surface feel meticulously crafted.

Use warm paper against deep ink, with one restrained orange accent and a pale green for an observed signal. Color accompanies labels rather than replacing them. The palette should carry the quiet precision of an instrument panel, with painstaking attention to contrast.

The rhythm comes from a small number of repeated rules and carefully spaced labels. Sans-serif type carries the main statement; monospaced detail records the evidence. Let the difference in scale communicate hierarchy without decorative effects or visual noise.

A subtle thread connects expectation with observation: the shared identifier is stable, while the evidence beneath it remains explicit. Keep that relationship legible through the arrangement itself. Refine spacing and proportions with the care of expert editorial work instead of adding more symbols.
